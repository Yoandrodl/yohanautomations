Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Is FlowPOS really 100% free? +

Yes, completely. FlowPOS Free Edition runs on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, both of which are free tools provided by Google. You never pay a monthly subscription fee or transaction commission.

Can I use my iPad as the cashier register? +

Absolutely! Once you deploy your web app link in Google Apps Script, simply open that link in Safari on your iPad, tap the Share icon, and select "Add to Home Screen". It opens as a full-screen, responsive POS app on your counter.

Where is my customer and sales data stored? +

Your data is stored 100% privately in your own Google Drive and Google Sheets. We have zero access to your data, transactions, customers, or financials.
